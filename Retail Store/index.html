Supply Chain & Retail Operations Reference Guide

Standard mathematical models and algorithms implemented in this project

Formula 1

Reorder Point (ROP)

ROP = (Average Daily Demand × Lead Time) + Safety Stock

Definition: The predetermined inventory level that signals the purchase of additional stock to avoid stockouts while waiting for supplier delivery.

  • Lead Time: Days required for delivery after placing order.
  • Safety Stock: Buffer stock to absorb unexpected demand spikes.
Formula 2

Economic Order Quantity (EOQ)

EOQ = √ [ (2 × D × S) / H ]

Definition: The ideal order quantity that minimizes the total annual inventory costs (ordering cost + holding/carrying cost).

  • D: Annual Demand (Units)
  • S: Fixed Order Cost per order ($)
  • H: Annual Holding Cost per unit ($)
Formula 3

Safety Stock (SS)

Safety Stock = (Max Daily Demand × Max Lead Time) - (Avg Daily Demand × Avg Lead Time)

Simplified Model: A buffer maintained to mitigate risks of stockouts caused by uncertainties in supply and demand.

Formula 4

Moving Average Demand Forecast

Forecast Demand (t+1) = (Sales Day 1 + ... + Sales Day N) / N

Definition: Smoothes short-term demand fluctuations and highlights baseline trends over a rolling 7-day sales window.

💡 How Replenishment Automation Works in this System:

Every time sales occur, the current stock decreases. The system constantly compares Current Stock against the calculated Reorder Point (ROP). When Current Stock ≤ ROP, the item is flagged with a warning. The automated replenishment order quantity defaults to the item's calculated EOQ (Economic Order Quantity) or replenishment up to the maximum warehouse target stock.
